Rules of the route

How to use this plan

Level bands are targets, not gates. Quest data is from beta and datamining; verify pickups on the live client.

Read each stage in order. Steps restart at 1 in every stage. Each stage says where you gain levels, what is only a quick errand, and when to leave. Checkboxes are saved in this browser only.

Your pet does the tanking

From 10 the pet holds the mob while you shoot. Keep it at your level (it only gains XP from kills), keep it fed and happy, and swap to a sturdier family around 16. In Forever pets scale with your gear: they inherit your crit and part of your Attack Power and armor.

Solo vs dungeons

Solo: pet in first, Hunter's Mark, Serpent Sting, Arcane Shot, auto shot. Dungeons: put Growl on passive or off, use Freezing Trap on the dangerous mob, Multi-Shot only once the tank has threat, and Feign Death from 30 if a pull goes wrong.

Ammo and bags

Ammo, a quiver or ammo pouch, and pet food all take bag space. As an Engineer you shoot guns and can craft your own bullets from Mining, so carry a stack of ore or bars instead of buying ammo everywhere. Upgrade your ranged weapon at the vendor at about 3, 11, 14 and 16.

Travel intelligently

Use Auberdine ↔ Stormwind, the one-way Menethil → Southshore → Auberdine → Menethil loop, and the Gadgetzan ↔ Riverglades port. Aspect of the Cheetah at 20 is your speed boost until the mount at 40.

Forever Hunter changes that shape the route: Aimed Shot is baseline at 20 and shares a cooldown with Multi-Shot; Arcane Shot now shares one with the new Beast Mastery talent Summon Hawk. Traps work in combat (30-second cooldown per school, one Fire and one Frost trap at a time). Disengage drops twice as much threat. Auto Shot needs you to stand still for half a second. Pets can't get player buffs any more, every family learns Bite or Claw plus Dive or Dash, and Foxes are a new family. Dungeon quests give half their old bonus XP, so questing carries more of the load.

Night Elf

  • Elune's Light: +10% crit for 15 seconds, for you and your pet.
  • Quickness: movement speed that stacks with Cheetah, plus dodge.
  • Shadowmeld: 10-second cooldown out of combat, so you can eat and drink hidden.
  • Start: Teldrassil, Darnassus as hub.

Stat priority

  • Agility first: Attack Power, crit, armor and dodge.
  • Hit up to about 9% against bosses at 60.
  • Attack Power, then crit (your pet inherits all of it).
  • Intellect and Spirit keep your mana up while leveling.

Taming and the pet plan

Which pet to use when, and how pets learn new abilities.

LVL 10

Taming the Beast

Dazalar, the Hunter trainer in Dolanaar, gives three quests. Each hands you a Taming Rod to tame one beast type: a spider, a nightsaber and a great forest owl in Teldrassil. Finishing all three teaches Tame Beast, Call Pet and Dismiss Pet.

LVL 10–11

Training the Beast

Talk to Jocaste in the Cenarion Enclave, Darnassus. He teaches Feed Pet and Revive Pet and unlocks Beast Training. Tame your keeper right after.

LVL 10

First pet

Guides recommend the Webwood Silkspinner in Teldrassil as the strongest early Night Elf pet. Spiders have their own unique ability (Web).

LVL ~16

Upgrade to a bear

Many Hunters switch to a bear around 16 for its health and armor. Darkshore and Ashenvale have bears near your level. Stable the spider if you like it.

TAMING RULES

Before you tame

You can only tame beasts at or below your level, with no active or dismissed pet. Your armor drops to zero while channeling, so drop a Freezing Trap or slow the beast first. Damaging it breaks the tame.

PET ABILITIES

Learning skills

Tame a beast that already knows a skill, fight with it until you see "You have learned a new spell", then teach it to your main pet with Beast Training. Dash comes this way. Up to three pets with two bought stable slots.

Leveling XP

Food buffs for faster leveling

Cooked food gives bonus XP from kills in Forever. Here is what to cook at each stage of this route.

In Forever most cooked food gives Well Fed: a stat buff plus 5% more experience from kills. It lasts about 15 minutes and drops when you die. The sources only mention kill XP, so it probably doesn't raise quest XP.

How the buff stacks

Rested XP doubles kill XP, the Cozy Sleeping Bag adds up to 3% XP, and a Leatherworker's Camp Tent gives rested XP for an hour. The Legacy talent Gourmand makes your food buffs last longer.

What changed in Forever

Recipes now favor one main stat each. Longjaw Mud Snapper and Smoked Sagefish became Attack Power or Spell Power food, so check the tooltip and eat the version that fits your class. Plain vendor food and water give no Well Fed buff, so no XP bonus either.

Spec & spells

Beast Mastery leveling build

All three specs are viable; Beast Mastery is the preferred leveling spec. Re-check level-60 raid builds at launch.

Talent path

  • 10–24: Endurance Training (pet health and armor), Focused Fire, Pathfinding
  • 25: Summon Hawk (up to two hawks, shares a cooldown with Arcane Shot)
  • Unleashed Fury and Bestial Swiftness
  • 30: Intimidation (next pet attack always crits)
  • 31+: Ferocity, Bestial Discipline, 4 points in Frenzy
  • 40: Bestial Wrath
  • Then Marksmanship: Lethal Attacks, Careful Aim, Trueshot Aura

Point totals change with Legacy talents; check the trainer's calculator at launch.

Pull pattern

Send the pet in and let it build threat. Hunter's Mark, Serpent Sting, then Arcane Shot and auto shot; from 25 keep two hawks out. Aimed Shot on single targets, Multi-Shot on packs. Raptor Strike if a mob reaches you, or Disengage. Keep Aspect of the Hawk up while fighting and Cheetah only while traveling.

Crafting, economy & gear

Mining + Engineering

Mining feeds Engineering. Engineering makes your gun, scopes, bullets and bombs.

Trainer recipes for a Hunter

  • Guns: Rough Boomstick (skill 50), Deadly Blunderbuss (105), Silver-Plated Shotgun (130), Mithril Blunderbuss (205).
  • Scopes: Crude Scope (60), Standard Scope (110), Ornate Spyglass (135). Accurate Scope (180) and Deadly Scope (210) come from vendors.
  • Bullets: Crafted Light Shot (30), Heavy Shot (75), Solid Shot (125), Hi-Impact Mithril Slugs (210).
  • Goggles: Flying Tiger Goggles (100), Green Tinted Goggles (150), Fire Goggles (205).
  • Drops worth watching: Moonsight Rifle (145), Mithril Heavy-bore Rifle (220), Sniper Scope (240), Thorium Shells (285).

Fritz Fizzle (Three Corners, Redridge)

  • 45 Merchant's Favor each: SAF-T Dynamite and Tabs (skill 25), SAF-T Copper Bomb (55), Satchel of Copper Bombs (85).
  • Then EZ-Thro Wrap (100) and EZ-Thro Copper Bomb XL (130).
  • Friendly, 180 Favor: Clanking Cord and Gizmo Girdle belts (175), four goggles (200).
  • Honored, 270–360 Favor: Emergency Field Cloak, Ultralight Goblin Glider.
  • Gnomish or Goblin specialization at 200 through a quest chain; switching later is expensive.
Satchel bombs use the thrown slot, which a Hunter's ranged weapon already fills. Before you spend Favor on them, check in game whether you can throw them while your gun is equipped. If not, put the Favor into belts and goggles instead.
