Rules of the route

How to use this plan

Level bands are targets, not gates. Quest data is from beta and datamining; verify pickups on the live client.

Read each stage in order. Steps restart at 1 in every stage. Each stage says where you gain levels, what is only a quick errand, and when to leave. Checkboxes are saved in this browser only.

Forms come from quests

Bear Form (10), Cure Poison (14), Aquatic Form (16) and, new in Forever, Cat Form (20) are class quests. All of them run through Mathrengyl Bearwalker in Darnassus and Dendrite Starblaze in Moonglade. Travel Form (30) and Dire Bear Form (40) come from the trainer.

Three roles, one route

Solo: Cat Form from 20 (Bear before that). Dungeons: tank in Bear Form with the hybrid build; groups form fastest around a tank. Balance: a viable but slower leveling spec. At 40 Dual Specialization lets you keep Feral and Balance (or a pure Bear build) side by side.

Never lose the quest item

The Aquatic Form needs two half pendants: one in northern Darkshore and one in Westfall, which you pick up on the Deadmines trip. Cure Poison needs 5 Earthroot; buy them if you don't have Herbalism. The Cat Form relics are party loot.

Travel intelligently

The Bear Form quest teaches Teleport: Moonglade; use it for every form quest. Use Auberdine ↔ Stormwind, the one-way Menethil → Southshore → Auberdine → Menethil loop, and later the Gadgetzan ↔ Riverglades port.

Forever Druid changes that shape the route: auto-attacks in Bear and Cat Form use your weapon's DPS, so weapon upgrades matter again. Most potions, weapon enchants and doors work without leaving form. Omen of Clarity is baseline at 20, Revive at 12, Nature's Grasp at 10, Lacerate at 42. Shifting Power replaces Tiger's Fury (40 Energy for mana), Primal Bite is a new high-threat bear attack, Thick Hide now scales with Defense gear, and combo points stay when you switch target. Druids still have no snare or interrupt, apart from Feral Charge in Bear Form.

Night Elf

  • Recommended Alliance race for every Druid spec.
  • Elune's Light (offense) and Quickness (defense).
  • Shadowmeld works while shapeshifted in Forever.
  • Start: Teldrassil, Darnassus as hub.

Pick a profession track at level 5

  • Track A, Skinning + Leatherworking: leather gear for all three roles from the Three Corners vendor, steady gold from leather.
  • Track B, Mining + Engineering: bombs that pull and AoE, goggles, belts, gliders; Gnomish or Goblin at 200.
  • Only tick the track you play in the Profession section.

LVL 10

Bear Form

Mathrengyl Bearwalker (Darnassus 35.4, 8.4) → Dendrite Starblaze (Moonglade 56.2, 30.6) → Great Bear Spirit (39.2, 27.5) → back to Darnassus → Body and Heart: Cenarion Moondust on the Moonkin Stone in the cave east of Auberdine (Darkshore 43.5, 46), defeat Lunaclaw.

Cure Poison

LVL 16

Aquatic Form

A Lesson to Learn → Dendrite. Trial of the Lake: Shrine Bauble from the bed of Lake Elune'ara (54.1, 50.1), use it at the Shrine of Remulos, talk to Tajarri (36.5, 40.1). Trial of the Sea Lion: half pendants in Westfall (17.9, 33.2) and northern Darkshore (48.9, 11.3).

LVL 16–18

Aquatic reward

Join the halves near the Shrine of Remulos, show Dendrite, then Mathrengyl. You get Aquatic Form, the Aquarius Belt and the relic-slot Idol of Shifting Tides.

LVL 20

Cat Form

The Great Cat Spirit → Dendrite → the spirit in southern Moonglade (54.6, 75.0) → three relics in the Stormrage Barrow Dens (entrance 71.2, 61.5). Click the statue at the door for five minutes of stealth. Back to Dendrite, then To Darnassus for Cat Form, Prowl, Claw and Rip.

Leveling XP

Food buffs for faster leveling

Cooked food gives bonus XP from kills in Forever. Here is what to cook at each stage of this route.

In Forever most cooked food gives Well Fed: a stat buff plus 5% more experience from kills. It lasts about 15 minutes and drops when you die. The sources only mention kill XP, so it probably doesn't raise quest XP.

How the buff stacks

Rested XP doubles kill XP, the Cozy Sleeping Bag adds up to 3% XP, and a Leatherworker's Camp Tent gives rested XP for an hour. The Legacy talent Gourmand makes your food buffs last longer.

What changed in Forever

Recipes now favor one main stat each. Longjaw Mud Snapper and Smoked Sagefish became Attack Power or Spell Power food, and Bristle Whisker Catfish and Rockscale Cod now raise Fishing skill. Every dish in the table also gives the +5% kill XP, so pick the one whose stat fits your class. Plain vendor food and water give no Well Fed buff, so no XP bonus either.

Crafting, economy & gear

Two profession tracks

Pick one at level 5 and only follow its checklist. Both vendors stand in Three Corners, Redridge, with the Merchant's Favor turn-in.

Track A recipes (Daniel Stitchsong)

  • 30 Favor each, no reputation: belts (skill 60), gloves (75), boots (85), hoods (100), tunics (110), legwear (125).
  • Six lines per slot: Brawler's, Defender's, Stormrider's, Totemic, Trapper's, Wisdom's. Check the tooltip and pick the one matching your role.
  • Friendly, 60 Favor: Mender's, Prowler's, Skulker's, Skycaller's and Warden's leather gloves (175) and shoulders (210), plus Forceful Thick Armor Kit (185).
  • Honored, 90 Favor: leather bracers (225), boots (235) and waistguards (250).

Track B recipes (Fritz Fizzle)

  • 45 Favor each: SAF-T Dynamite and Tabs (skill 25), SAF-T Copper Bomb (55), Satchel of Copper Bombs (85, BoP thrown-slot AoE with no ammo), EZ-Thro Wrap (100), EZ-Thro Copper Bomb XL (130).
  • Friendly, 180 Favor: Satchel of Bronze Bombs (165), Clanking Cord and Gizmo Girdle belts (175), four goggles (200).
  • Honored, 270–360 Favor: Satchel of Iron and Dark Iron Bombs, Emergency Field Cloak, Ultralight Goblin Glider (300).
  • Gnomish or Goblin specialization at 200 via a quest chain; switching later is expensive.
Merchant's Favor: loot Waylaid Crates while questing, fill them and hand them to Marcy Baker in Three Corners (your first crate also starts a quest worth 50 Favor). Gold reserve: hold about 100g at 40 for Riding. Druids have no class mount, so this is a real cost.
