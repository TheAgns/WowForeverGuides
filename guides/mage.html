Rules of the route

How to use this plan

Level bands are targets, not gates. Quest data is from beta and datamining; verify pickups on the live client.

Read each stage in order. Steps restart at 1 in every stage. Each stage says where you gain levels, what is only a quick errand, and when to leave. Checkboxes are saved in this browser only.

Robes, wands and staves

Mage class quests reward gear: an Arcane Staff at 10, the Manaweave Robe at 15, the Astral Knot Robe at 26, a wand and a Celestial staff around 40, and the Fire Ruby at 52. Each one is placed on a trip you already make.

Solo vs dungeons

Solo: Frostbolt, Frost Nova, wand, and Ice Lance on frozen targets. Dungeons: Polymorph the dangerous pull, then Blizzard or Arcane Explosion once the tank has threat. You also conjure food and water for the whole group.

Never lose the quest item

Ur's Treatise sits in the Tower of Ilgalar in Redridge; take the quest before you go. The Mage's Wand needs a book from the SM Library while the quest is active. Morphaz in Sunken Temple only counts after the Azshara steps.

Travel intelligently

From 20 you teleport to capitals; from 40 you sell portals for gold. Use Stormwind ↔ Auberdine, the one-way Menethil → Southshore → Auberdine → Menethil loop, and the Gadgetzan ↔ Riverglades port.

Forever Mage changes that shape the route: Ice Lance is a new Frost talent (big damage on frozen targets, now with an 8-second cooldown) and Fingers of Frost lets you use it without a Nova. Frostfire Bolt arrives at 40. Improved Blizzard's slow is capped at 40% and Cone of Cold is shorter, which deliberately weakens mass AoE farming. Cold Snap is now the 21-point talent with a 10-minute cooldown. Comprehension is a new Mage-only skill: read dropped scrolls with Comprehend Scroll for temporary buffs.

Gnome

  • Eureka!: 8 seconds of cheaper, harder-hitting spells. Strong with Ice Lance, Blizzard and Arcane Explosion.
  • Expansive Mind: +5% mana.
  • Escape Artist for roots and snares.
  • Start: Dun Morogh, Ironforge hub.

Stat priority

  • Early: Intellect and Spell Damage, then Stamina and Spirit.
  • From 30: Spell Damage and Intellect, then Mp5 and Stamina.
  • Caster weapons now lean on Spell Damage; a good wand saves a lot of mana.
  • Weapon oils (Minor/Lesser Wizard Oil) from Enchanting.

LVL 10

Mage-tastic Gizmonitor

Bink in Ironforge. The Gizmonitor is in a chest on the ground floor of the two-story hut north of Gnomeregan (Dun Morogh 27, 36); clear the gnomes first. Pick the Arcane Staff (or Orb).

LVL 15

Manaweave Robe

Report to Jennea (Stormwind) → Investigate the Blue Recluse (Mage Quarter inn) → Gathering Materials → Manaweave Robe. Do it on a Westfall/Redridge pass through Stormwind.

LVL 26

Astral Knot Garment

High Sorcerer Andromath (Stormwind) → Ur's Treatise on Shadow Magic from Morganth's library in the Tower of Ilgalar, Redridge → Pristine Spider Silk in Duskwood → Wynne Larson, Larson Clothiers.

LVL 38–41

Mage's Wand

Tabetha (Dustwallow ~43, 57) → Magus Tirth, Shimmering Flats (~77, 76) → /beckon the chicken Plucky Johnson → book in the SM Library Athenaeum → 10 Witherbark Totem Sticks (Arathi ~65, 65) and a Jade → Icefury, Nether Force or Ragefire Wand.

LVL 40–43

Celestial Power

Return to the Marsh → The Infernal Orb → The Exorcism (elite) → Power in Uldaman → Mana Surges → Celestial Stave or Orb. Uldaman is already on the route at 42.

Magecraft → Magic Dust and The Siren's Coral in Azshara → Destroy Morphaz in Sunken Temple: Fire Ruby. At 60, Lorekeeper Lydros in Dire Maul North: Hydrospawn Essence for the top Conjure Water.

Leveling XP

Food buffs for faster leveling

Cooked food gives bonus XP from kills in Forever. Here is what to cook at each stage of this route.

In Forever most cooked food gives Well Fed: a stat buff plus 5% more experience from kills. It lasts about 15 minutes and drops when you die. The sources only mention kill XP, so it probably doesn't raise quest XP.

How the buff stacks

Rested XP doubles kill XP, the Cozy Sleeping Bag adds up to 3% XP, and a Leatherworker's Camp Tent gives rested XP for an hour. The Legacy talent Gourmand makes your food buffs last longer.

What changed in Forever

Recipes now favor one main stat each. Longjaw Mud Snapper and Smoked Sagefish became Attack Power or Spell Power food, so check the tooltip and eat the version that fits your class. Plain vendor food and water give no Well Fed buff, so no XP bonus either.

Spec & spells

Frost leveling build

All three specs are viable; Frost has the best survivability and AoE. Re-check level-60 raid builds at launch.

Talent path

  • 10–15: Improved Frostbolt (faster casts)
  • Frostbite / Permafrost for roots and slows
  • 11 points: Ice Lance
  • Row 5: Fingers of Frost (Ice Lance without a Nova)
  • 16 points: Ice Block; 21 points: Cold Snap
  • Improved Blizzard for group AoE
  • 40: second spec via Dual Specialization (e.g. Fire or Arcane for raids)

Point totals change with Legacy talents; check the trainer's calculator at launch.

Pull pattern

Frostbolt, Frostbolt, Frost Nova when it reaches you, step back, Ice Lance on the frozen target, wand to finish. Two mobs: Polymorph one. Keep Frost Armor (later Ice Armor) and Arcane Intellect up, and drink conjured water between pulls. Hold mana for Nova and Blink if a pull goes wrong.

Crafting, economy & gear

Tailoring + Enchanting

Cloth from questing feeds Tailoring; Tailoring greens feed Enchanting; Enchanting makes your wands and oils.

Tailoring

  • Tailors find extra cloth on humanoids in Forever; bank linen, wool and silk.
  • Mivin Shadowweave in Three Corners, Redridge sells BoP patterns for 30 Merchant's Favor each. Guides point to the Silky line for Frost (Flame for Fire): Boots (skill 60), Gloves (75), Sash (85), Circlet (100), Gown (110), Leggings (125).
  • Filigreed Silky Gown (skill 75) drops from Rhahk'Zor in Deadmines, a dungeon already on your route. Filigreed Silky Leggings (100) also come from dungeon drops.
  • At Friendly with the Azeroth Commerce Authority: 60-Favor patterns from skill 165. Check tooltips for the frost variant.

Enchanting

  • Disenchant every Tailoring green and unwanted BoE.
  • Wands: Novice's Practice Wand and Lesser Magic Wand at skill 10, Greater Magic Wand around 70.
  • Alynsia in Three Corners sells formulas for Merchant's Favor: Glimmering Staff and Enchant Weapon – Insight (skill 140, 45 Favor), Enchant Necklace – Spell Power (210, 120), Dreamstaff (220, 120), Brilliant Wand (275, 180).
  • Minor and Lesser Wizard Oil for dangerous pulls.
