01 / Rules of the route

How to use this plan

Level bands are targets, not hard gates. Verify quest pickup/loot on the live client.

Read each stage in order. The numbered checkbox steps reset to 1 in every stage. Each stage tells you where to gain levels, what is only a quick travel errand, and when to leave. For instance: level 20–23 = quest Redridge; Loch Modan = just Jordan’s ore. Check boxes as you go.

Quest first, dungeon second

Pick up the important dungeon quests and class components before committing to a run. Forever rebalanced dungeon XP in October: the initial quest bonus was reduced, and kill XP was increased. Well-prepared clears still matter, but the best XP/hour will depend on your group and travel.

Two equipment tracks

Open world: efficient slow 2H weapon — Ironforge Greathammer → Verigan's Fist → Heavehammer / Defender of Dalaran if better. Dungeons: shield + 1H, Righteous Fury active, use your Holy threat abilities.

Never lose the quest item

Hall of Thanes needs the Dark Iron Map → Old Ironforge Incursion. Deadmines needs The Test of Righteousness before its lumber; VanCleef's Unsent Letter sets up Stockades. BFD's Kor Gem starts with Thundris in Auberdine.

Travel intelligently

Use Stormwind ↔ Auberdine and the one-way Menethil → Southshore → Auberdine → Menethil loop. The neutral Gadgetzan ↔ Riverglades port link helps later. Record flight paths.

As of the October 30 hotfix: Righteous Fury is moved to level 10 in client/hotfix tracking. Hall of Thanes at level 14 is the planned early tank run, subject to its quest chain being available in the launch build. The plan reflects beta data, not an immutable launch guarantee.

02 / The opening journey

Levels 1–26

The most tightly scripted part of the route: Greathammer, Sleeping Bag, Crusader helm, Verigan's Fist and Stockades.

LVL 01–10Elwynn / Stormwind

Elwynn Forest — establish the economy

Level in Northshire and Elwynn. Use a solid two-handed weapon for solo quests. Take Mining + Skinning as soon as practical; bank the ore and most stone. Sell leather for cash, but reserve four Pristine Leather for your future helm.

QUEST HERENorthshire, then Goldshire/Elwynn Forest
TRAVEL / ERRAND ONLYStormwind only for convenient trainers and mail.
MOVE ON WHENReach level 10, then head for Westfall.
LVL 10–14Westfall / Stormwind / Elwynn

Westfall + the Redemption class quest

Begin Defias quest progression now so it feeds naturally into Deadmines later. At 12, do the Tome of Divinity chain for Redemption. Keep the hearthstone convenient for Stormwind.

QUEST HEREWestfall: Sentinel Hill, farms, Defias chains
TRAVEL / ERRAND ONLYStormwind + eastern Elwynn briefly for Redemption at 12.
MOVE ON WHENStart Sleeping Bag at 14, then take tram to Ironforge.
LVL 14Dun Morogh / Old Ironforge

Hall of Thanes — get Ironforge Greathammer

This is our first dungeon and the first key weapon reward. Righteous Fury is now learned at 10 in late beta data, so level 14 is the plan. The must-have quest is Old Ironforge Incursion, which requires the Underground Map prerequisite. It rewards Ironforge Greathammer from King Magni.

QUEST HERENo separate questing zone: dungeon + prerequisites
TRAVEL / ERRAND ONLYDun Morogh only for Earthseer Farsen / Dark Iron Map; Ironforge for quest pickups and rewards.
MOVE ON WHENFinish Hall of Thanes at 14; claim Greathammer, then head to Stormwind docks.

Routing check: If a prerequisite is gated at 14 on launch, do nearby quests to 15 first. Do not burn a full run without the Greathammer quest.

LVL ~14–17 (TRAVEL)Westfall → Barrens → Stonetalon → Loch Modan → Thoradin's Wall

Cozy Sleeping Bag expedition

This stage is an expedition for the Sleeping Bag, not a normal 14–19 XP grind. Start it after the level-14 Hall of Thanes run; use the Stormwind–Auberdine and Menethil–Southshore boat routes to reduce travel. You may gain some XP from the chain, but finish any remaining levels to 20 in Westfall and then Redridge afterward.

QUEST HERETravel/quest-chain stage; do not grind another zone
TRAVEL / ERRAND ONLYBarrens, Stonetalon, Loch Modan dam and Hillsbrad are Sleeping Bag stops, not leveling hubs.
MOVE ON WHENFinish Sleeping Bag, then QUEST Westfall/Redridge for whatever XP is still needed before level 20.

Routing check: This crosses Horde territory. Avoid guard camps and don't force it at the expense of many corpse runs.

LVL ~16–20Westfall / Redridge / Stormwind

Westfall / Redridge — the level-20 staging area

After the Sleeping Bag travel, resume actual XP questing: finish efficient Westfall/Defias chains and use Redridge for the rest of the push to 20. Prepare Deadmines quests and helm materials; no need for full Loch Modan questing here. Merchant's Favor and the recipe are separate from gathering materials.

QUEST HEREWestfall until Defias work is done; then Redridge for remaining XP
TRAVEL / ERRAND ONLYStormwind for trainer, supplies and Deadmines quests; do NOT start a full Loch Modan quest circuit.
MOVE ON WHENReach 20 with materials/Favor ready for Blacksmithing + Paladin chain.
LVL 20Redridge trade camp / anvil

Profession swap — craft Crusader's Silvered Chain Helm

Drop Skinning and immediately learn Blacksmithing. Raise BS to 95 with banked copper/tin/stone. Buy Plans: Crusader's Silvered Chain Helm from Stondry Darkhammer for 30 Merchant's Favor at the Azeroth Commerce Authority camp near Three Corners in Redridge (not a normal gold vendor). Craft at an anvil. This helmet is BoP and requires level 20.

QUEST HERENo quest grinding: short level-20 crafting stop
TRAVEL / ERRAND ONLYRedridge trade camp for recipe; Stormwind/Ironforge trainers, bank and anvil as convenient.
MOVE ON WHENEquip helm when possible, then proceed with Tome of Valor.

Routing check: Check launch availability and the recipe purchase before dropping Skinning. If you lack the Favor or silver, postpone crafting briefly instead of losing hours of questing.

LVL 20Stormwind → Westfall → Ironforge/Jordan → Loch Modan ore → Deadmines

Tome of Valor → Jordan → grab Loch Modan ore before Deadmines

At level 20, start Tome of Valor in Stormwind, defend Daphne in Westfall, return to Duthorian, then get The Test of Righteousness from Jordan outside Ironforge. Because you are already there, make a quick Thelsamar → Mo'grosh → Thelsamar trip for the Refined Ore Shipment BEFORE returning for Deadmines. Mo'grosh involves elite ogres, so use a group or defer only that errand if it would waste time. This order saves an otherwise redundant Ironforge/Loch Modan round trip.

TRAVEL / ERRAND ONLYStormwind → Westfall lighthouse → Stormwind → Jordan near Ironforge → Thelsamar/Mo'grosh → Thelsamar.
MOVE ON WHENBring refined ore back and head to Deadmines with Test of Righteousness active.

Routing check: Do not assume the weapon component drops before the proper Paladin quest is active.

Do one dungeon-quest-loaded Shadowfang Keep run rather than a special low-level break-in. Your Southshore flight path and Forever boat route help reduce travel. Find Jordan's Smithing Hammer in the stables/courtyard.

Blackfathom Deeps + purified Kor Gem

Use the Stormwind–Auberdine ship. First talk to Thundris Windweaver in Auberdine and get Seeking the Kor Gem. Kill naga near the BFD approach for Corrupted Kor Gem (can be done outside the instance); turn it in to purify. We still want one real BFD tank run for its quests and experience.

Claim Verigan's Fist + Stockades

Leveling XP

Food buffs for faster leveling

Cooked food gives bonus XP from kills in Forever. Here is what to cook at each stage of this route.

In Forever most cooked food gives Well Fed: a stat buff plus 5% more experience from kills. It lasts about 15 minutes and drops when you die. The sources only mention kill XP, so it probably doesn't raise quest XP.

How the buff stacks

Rested XP doubles kill XP, the Cozy Sleeping Bag adds up to 3% XP, and a Leatherworker's Camp Tent gives rested XP for an hour. The Legacy talent Gourmand makes your food buffs last longer.

What changed in Forever

Recipes now favor one main stat each. Longjaw Mud Snapper and Smoked Sagefish became Attack Power or Spell Power food, and Bristle Whisker Catfish and Rockscale Cod now raise Fishing skill. Every dish in the table also gives the +5% kill XP, so pick the one whose stat fits your class. Plain vendor food and water give no Well Fed buff, so no XP bonus either.

07 / Crafting, economy & gear

Profession swap at 20

Profit early; craft a self-only tank helm; reserve enough gold for riding uncertainty.

Levels 1–19 · Mining + Skinning

  • Bank: copper, tin, silver, stone and useful intermediate materials for Blacksmithing.
  • Sell: most leather/hides, spare green drops, vendor trash.
  • Keep: at least four Pristine Leather for Crusader's helm.
  • Mage bank alt: Tailoring for bags, Enchanting for early Lesser Magic Essences, storage and AH.

Level 20 · Mine + Blacksmith

  • Drop Skinning, train BS, raise it to skill 95.
  • Get 30 Merchant's Favor on the Paladin and buy the Crusader helm recipe from Stondry Darkhammer in Redridge.
  • Craft the BoP item at an anvil. From then on your profession pair stays Mining + Blacksmithing.
  • Don't spend hours power-leveling BS beyond a useful stopping point during the race to 60.
