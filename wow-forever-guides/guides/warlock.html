Rules of the route

How to use this plan

Level bands are targets, not gates. Quest data below is from beta and datamining; verify pickups on the live client.

Read each stage in order. Steps restart at 1 in every stage. Each stage says where you gain levels, what is only a quick errand, and when to leave. Checkboxes are saved in this browser only.

Demons come from quests

In Forever a Warlock starts with no demon. The Imp is a short starting-zone quest, the Voidwalker comes at 10, Succubus or Incubus at 20, Felhunter at 30. Do each demon quest the day you reach its level; leveling without the Voidwalker is much slower.

Two pet tracks

Solo: Voidwalker tanks, you stack DoTs and wand. Dungeons: Imp for Blood Pact and Fire Shield, or Succubus for damage; Voidwalker only if the group lacks a steady tank. Keep one Soul Shard spare at all times for resummons.

Never lose the quest item

The Soran'ruk staff needs 3 fragments from BFD and 1 from SFK: accept Doan Karhan's quest at 20 before either run. Felhunter Rods of Channeling only drop while the quest is active. Deadmines still drops VanCleef's Unsent Letter for Stockades.

Travel intelligently

Use Stormwind ↔ Auberdine, the one-way Menethil → Southshore → Auberdine → Menethil loop, and the neutral Gadgetzan ↔ Riverglades port. Take the Ratchet flight path early: Succubus, Soran'ruk, Felhunter and Felsteed all pass through the Barrens.

Forever Warlock changes that shape the route: Agony is now Bane of Agony (banes no longer count as curses, so keep a curse up too); DoTs can crit; Drain Soul can give a shard on any tick; demon-quest items are party loot with rising drop chance; the Staff of Soran'ruk is now rare (+14 Stamina, +34 Shadow/Fire damage). Whether the Felsteed quest also grants the Riding skill is unconfirmed, so this plan keeps a 100g reserve at 40.

Gnome

  • Expansive Mind: +5% mana.
  • Eureka!: cheaper, stronger direct spells for 8s. It does not affect DoTs, so it is weak for Affliction.
  • Escape Artist for snares.
  • Start: Dun Morogh, Ironforge hub.

Human

  • Ruthless: +2% crit, and DoTs can crit in Forever. Best Alliance pick for Affliction in current guides.
  • Extra Spirit, which mainly helps Life Tap.
  • Will to Survive, a strong PvP racial.
  • Start: Northshire, Stormwind hub.

The opening journey

Levels 10–26

Voidwalker, Westfall, Hall of Thanes, the level-20 Kalimdor loop, SFK and BFD for the staff, then Stockades.

Class weapon & demon campaign

Succubus + Soran'ruk, no wasted trips

Takar the Seer and Doan Karhan stand next to each other near Camp Taurajo, so one trip covers both.

Why the Sleeping Bag waits until 20: its Barrens and Stonetalon stops sit on the same road as Takar the Seer. Folding them into the level-20 loop saves a full extra Kalimdor crossing; the Loch Modan stop fits the Hall of Thanes trip and Thoradin's Wall fits the SFK trip through Southshore.

Gakin the Darkbinder gives Devourer of Souls. Gnomes get the lead from Lago Blackwrench in Ironforge first. Train Rain of Fire and Ritual of Summoning.

02 · LVL 20

Barrens ~49, 57

Talk to Takar the Seer and accept The Orb of Soran'ruk from Doan Karhan beside him. Sleeping Bag clue south of Camp Taurajo (~46.4, 73.9).

03 · LVL 20

Ashenvale 31.4, 31.5

Heartswood at Ordil'Aran: click the roots. Incubus instead: Wooden Figurine in the house at ~26, 22 (light the torch, burn the cart).

04 · LVL 20–21

Stormwind binding

Use the item at the circle below the Slaughtered Lamb with the Voidwalker out first. Reward: demon + Small Soul Pouch.

05 · LVL 22–25

SFK + BFD

1 Large Soran'ruk Fragment from Shadowfang Darksoul (shadow immune: Immolate and wand). 3 Soran'ruk Fragments from Twilight Acolytes in BFD.

06 · LVL 24–25

Doan Karhan

Turn in for the Staff of Soran'ruk (Stamina, safer for leveling) or the Orb if you already hold a strong 1H. Use the Auberdine trip after BFD.

Spec & spells

Affliction leveling build

All three specs are viable; Affliction has the lowest downtime. Re-check level-60 raid builds at launch.

Talent order

  • 4 Suppression (never miss same-level mobs)
  • 5 Improved Corruption (instant Corruption)
  • 1 more Suppression
  • Pandemic (DoT crits deal double)
  • Improved Bane of Agony, then Malevolence
  • 30: Siphon Life, then Fel Concentration
  • 34: 1 Soul Siphon, 5 Shadow Mastery
  • 40: Wrack capstone

Pull pattern

Send the Voidwalker in, then Immolate, Corruption and Bane of Agony (Bane tags instantly in crowded zones). Wand to finish; Drain Life only when you need health. Life Tap for mana and eat. Keep Demon Skin / Demon Armor up. Drain Soul on mobs you need shards from.

Crafting, economy & gear

Tailoring + Enchanting

Cloth from questing feeds Tailoring; Tailoring greens feed Enchanting; Enchanting makes your wands.

Tailoring

  • Tailors find extra cloth on humanoids in Forever, so bank linen, wool and silk instead of selling it.
  • Mivin Shadowweave in Three Corners, Redridge sells BoP Shadow patterns for 30 Merchant's Favor each: Boots (skill 60), Gloves (75), Sash (85), Circlet (100), Gown (110), Leggings (125).
  • Filigreed Shadow Gown (skill 75, level 15) drops in Wailing Caverns; buy the pattern on the AH.
  • At Friendly with the Azeroth Commerce Authority: 60-Favor patterns from skill 165 (handwraps, Netherflame shoulders, cuffs, waistcords).

Enchanting

  • Disenchant every Tailoring green and unwanted BoE.
  • Lesser Magic Wand at skill 10, Greater Magic Wand around skill 70: your first two wands.
  • Merchant's Favor formulas: Orb of Souls (skill 140, 45 Favor), Enchant Necklace – Spell Power (210, 120 Favor), Brilliant Wand (275, 180 Favor).
  • Keep crafting to a useful stopping point; don't stall leveling for skill-ups.
